QUESTIONS YOU'LL ASK

Yes, we asked them too.

Will Google penalize AI-generated content?+
No — Google penalizes unhelpful content. Ralf's articles are researched, fact-checked, and written in your voice, citing primary sources. We're not in the business of churn-and-burn.
Can I review articles before they publish?+
Yes. Auto-publish is opt-in. By default everything lands in a queue for your sign-off — articles, pitches, and outreach replies all flow through the inbox.
What's AEO and why should I care?+
AEO is Answer Engine Optimization — getting your site cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google's AI Overviews. AI search is already 10–20% of the queries that used to hit Google, and it's growing fast. Ralf optimises for both at once.
Do I need an existing website?+
Yes, Ralf needs a site to optimise. If you're pre-launch, start with a one-page site and Ralf will grow with you.
What if I'm in a technical or regulated niche?+
Ralf can be primed on your specific docs, style guides, and compliance constraints — most niches work great. Drop us a line if you're not sure.
How long until I see results?+
First article ships in 24 hours. Ranking traction typically shows up between weeks 4–8.
What does Ralf cost vs. an agency?+
Roughly 1/10th the cost — and Ralf runs every day, not once a quarter.
