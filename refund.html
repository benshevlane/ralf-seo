Refund Policy

Last updated: 19 June 2026

We want you to feel confident using Ralf. Here's how refunds work:

New subscribers

If you're not satisfied within your first 7 days, contact us for a full refund, no questions asked.

Annual plan subscribers

You have 14 days from your start date to request a full refund.

Renewal refunds

If you're billed for a renewal and didn't mean to continue, contact us within 1 day of the renewal charge and we'll refund it in full.

Everything else

All other charges are non-refundable. You can cancel at any time and retain access until the end of your billing period.

To request a refund, open the chat in your Ralf account and say "I'd like a refund". We aim to process all requests within 24 hours.
