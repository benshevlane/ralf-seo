FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Can I use this alongside my existing SEO agency?
Yes. Your agency can continue handling strategy, content and technical SEO while Ralf handles placement on relevant pages where competitors already appear. The workstreams sit alongside each other.
What if I do not like an opportunity?
Skip it. The placement is not deducted from your budget unless you approve the exact article and price. Unused budget can roll over for up to three months.
How many links will I get for $500?
Typically one to two, depending on the publishers available and the strength of the opportunity. Most placements are expected to land around $300–$500, but the exact article and price are always shown before approval.
Are these paid placements?
Some are. Real publications with editorial standards and meaningful traffic often charge for commercial placement. Ralf is explicit about the cost and gives you the page data before you decide.
What happens to unspent budget?
It rolls over for up to three months, allowing you to save toward a larger placement instead of forcing a weaker opportunity into a particular month.
Do I have to commit long term?
No. The service is month to month. You can cancel future renewals at any time, and each individual placement still requires your approval.
