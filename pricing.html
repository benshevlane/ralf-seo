The models behind your score

We tell you exactly which AI models we check — most tools won’t.

Your visibility score is only as honest as the models behind it. We run the model that matches what real people see on each assistant, publish every one, and update them as they change.

ChatGPT · GPT-5.4
~77% of AI search — frontier-class accuracy where it matters most
Google · Gemini 3.5 Flash
the #2 surface — near-Pro quality
Claude · Claude Sonnet 4.6
Anthropic’s workhorse model — what most Claude research queries actually run on
Perplexity · Sonar
the search-native answer engine

GPT-backed and ~3% standalone share — proxied efficiently

full coverage of X’s assistant

We put the strongest models on the engines people actually search on — ChatGPT and Google are ~80% of AI search — and proxy the low-share surfaces efficiently. So your score is accurate where it counts, and you’re not paying for noise. Most tools will tell you they check “ChatGPT” — never which model. That’s the difference between a score you can trust and a number quietly inflated by a weaker model.

What does the Suite include?
Search, Content and Outreach at the matched tier, billed as one. From $137 a month — all three products, billed as one.
How do markets (countries) work?
Your weekly prompt allowance is a shared pool across the markets you track — it is not multiplied per market. With 2 markets your 150 prompts/week become 75 in each; with 3, about 50 each. Every market gets its own visibility score, and adding a market splits your coverage rather than increasing your bill.
Which CMS do I need for Content?
Auto-publish is live and proven on WordPress, Webflow and GitHub — these are the three we run in production today. Wix works but is in early testing, so we'll set it up with you personally. On a custom stack, talk to us: a generic webhook or self-hosted publish endpoint can work. And if you'd rather not connect anything at all, you don't have to — Ralf still researches and writes every article and gives you the finished post to paste in yourself. The CMS connection buys you the publishing step, not the writing.
Do I need Instantly.ai for Outreach?
For fully hands-off sending, yes. Ralf sends and follows up through your own Instantly.ai account — you connect your API key, and your emails go out from your domain and your sending reputation, not ours. Without Instantly, Outreach still does the hard part: it finds the sites AI engines trust, verifies the contact, and writes every email. It just hands you the copy and the recipient, and you press send. Instantly is billed by Instantly, separately from your Ralf plan.
Can I change plans later?
Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or add products at any time — changes apply from your next billing cycle.
