Agency FAQ

The questions agencies ask first.

Do my clients ever see Ralf?

No. White-label mode puts your logo, colours and web address on the dashboard and every report. Clients see your agency.

Can I stop Ralf publishing without my say-so?

Yes. Per client you choose autonomous or approval-required. In approval mode, nothing touches a client's live site until you tap approve in the queue. Some actions — like removing existing content or first publish to a new CMS — always ask, whatever the setting.

How is a client counted?

Per website. A multi-domain brand counts as one client per domain. Billing is month-to-month with no lock-in.

What do I actually resell?

The whole AI-search outcome: visibility tracking across six engines, AI-readiness fixes, answer-optimised content, technical Site Health, and reporting — delivered under your brand. You price and own the client relationship.

How do I get started?

We onboard agency partners directly at first. Book a demo and we'll set up your account, your branding, and your first client together.
