How it works · three things, on repeat

Find the opportunity. Write the pitch. Chase the reply.

1 · it finds

Ralf asks the AI engines the questions your buyers ask, and records who gets named. Then it looks at who links to your competitors — and not to you. Those are your openings.

2 · it writes

A real email to a real person, referencing the actual page it wants the link on. Not a template with your name pasted in. You read it before it goes.

3 · it sends, and keeps going

Sent from your own mailbox, so replies come to you. Ralf follows up, handles the back-and-forth, and tells you when a link lands. It also writes and publishes the content the whole thing hangs on. You approve; Ralf chases.
