Guidance for AI agents and assistants reading or navigating this site.

## What this site is

Ralf (ralfhq.com) is the marketing site for **Ralf**, an agentic SEO / AEO platform
that tracks whether AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Overviews,
Grok, Copilot) name or cite a business, then does the work to improve it: technical
fixes, SERP-grounded content, and backlink/citation outreach. The product itself
runs at **app.ralfhq.com**.
