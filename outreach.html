FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Does Ralf email people without my approval?
Only if you want it to. Ralf has autonomy settings: keep it in approval mode and every pitch and follow-up waits for your yes — or switch on auto mode and Ralf sends and follows up on its own, within the limits and sending identity you set. Either way, every message is logged for you to review.
How does Ralf find the right contacts?
It looks for citation and backlink openings in your category — broken-link reclaim, unlinked mentions, roundups and resource pages — finds the real person to reach, and checks the domain is worth your time before drafting anything.
What stops it pitching spammy link sellers?
Every target gets a link-risk check with a Safe / Review / High verdict from fresh data, and known pay-to-play domains are flagged before you ever send. You only chase links worth earning.
Does it handle replies and other languages?
Yes. Replies come into one queue, classified and ready to action, and Ralf drafts the next step. It writes outbound in the recipient's language and translates their replies back to English.
