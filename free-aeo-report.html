Questions

What is an AEO / AI visibility report?

AEO — Answer Engine Optimisation — is SEO for AI assistants. The report shows whether AI tools like ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews mention your business when people ask for recommendations, which is fast becoming how buyers find companies.

Is it really free?

Yes. The report runs a live scan across all six engines at no cost and with no card. You only pay if you decide to have Ralf work on improving your visibility afterwards.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You enter your website and the report runs straight away. We'll email you a link so you can come back to it, and you can create an account later if you want Ralf to act on the findings.

How accurate is it?

The scan queries the real AI engines live and reads your actual site content, so it reflects what buyers see today — not a cached guess. Results naturally shift as the engines update, which is why ongoing tracking matters.
