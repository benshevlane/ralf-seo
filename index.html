QUESTIONS YOU'LL ASK

Yes, we asked them too.

What's AEO and why should I care?+
AEO is Answer Engine Optimization — getting your site cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google's AI Overviews. AI search is already 10–20% of the queries that used to hit Google, and it's growing fast. The traditional SEO stack doesn't see any of it. Ralf does.
How is Ralf different from Ahrefs or Semrush?+
Ahrefs and Semrush are excellent at the old job — Google rank tracking and backlink data. They're blind to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and AI Overviews. Ralf is built for the AI-search era from day one, and pairs that with a modern technical SEO crawler so you don't have to pay for two tools.
What's coming after Search?+
Content (AI-drafted articles, on-brand, auto-published) and Outreach (backlink prospecting + personalised pitches + reply management) are next. Both are functionally built — we're launching Search first so we can deliver each product properly. Search subscribers get first access when they launch.
Do I need an existing website?+
Yes — Ralf needs a site to scan. If you're pre-launch, start with a one-page site and Ralf will grow with you.
How long until I see useful insight?+
First scan completes within an hour of connecting. You'll have a full AI Visibility baseline and Site Health report inside the first day, with weekly improvement reports after that.
Will Ralf fix my SEO issues for me?+
Today: Ralf identifies issues, explains them, and gives you a prioritised fix list. When Content launches, Ralf will fix the writeable ones (on-page copy, schema, internal links) automatically — for sites that opt in. Customers under your control, always.
How does the free trial work?+
Sign up, add your site, and you get 14 days of Ralf Search free — no card required. If it's useful, pick a tier; if not, walk away and you've paid nothing. Annual billing gets you 2 months free.
