What does Frase.io actually do?

Frase is a content optimization platform. It scrapes the top-ranking pages for a keyword, extracts the topics and questions they cover, and builds a brief so your writer hits the same terms. Then it grades your draft against that target as you write.

It does this well. Frase is affordable, the interface is clean, and for teams producing volume content aimed at classic Google rankings, it earns its place. The credit system draws complaints, and the AI writer is average — but the brief and grading workflow is the reason people pay.

Here's the catch. Everything Frase optimizes for is built on SERP data: what ranks on page one of Google. That's a proxy for quality, not a measurement of whether an AI model will cite you.

What is the difference between SEO and AEO optimization?

SEO optimizes a page to rank in a list of blue links. AEO — answer engine optimization — optimizes content so AI models quote it directly in a generated answer, where there's no list and often no click.

The mechanics diverge more than most teams expect. Google ranks pages. AI models assemble answers from fragments — a sentence here, a statistic there, a definition from somewhere else. To get cited, you need content that's structured for extraction: direct answers, clear entities, schema, and quotable data. A Frase brief tuned to word count and keyword coverage doesn't guarantee any of that.

And critically, ranking well on Google no longer means you'll appear in AI answers. The two overlap, but they're not the same game. We cover the split in detail in Google SEO vs ChatGPT optimization.

How do AI models decide which sources to reference?

AI models pull from a mix of their training data, real-time retrieval, and the wider web of mentions and citations about your brand. They favour sources that are clearly structured, frequently referenced by others, and unambiguous about what you are and do.

This is where the Frase model runs out of road. Getting cited depends heavily on off-page signals — how often credible sites mention you, whether your entity is consistent across the web, how much authority backs your claims. A content grader can't see those signals, let alone fix them. It optimizes the words on one page. AI citation is decided by a much larger surface area.

That's the gap dedicated AEO platforms exist to close.

What do dedicated AEO automation platforms do differently?

A dedicated AEO platform starts from a question Frase never asks: are you actually showing up in AI answers right now? It runs your brand and competitors through the major engines, records where you're cited and where you're absent, and identifies the specific gaps holding you back.

Then — and this is the real divergence — the better platforms execute the fixes. Ralf, for example, tracks your visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity, finds the citation and backlink gaps against competitors, and automatically publishes content, adjusts site structure, and runs outreach to close them. You're not handed a brief to action later. The work gets done.

That's a fundamentally different product category. One is a research and writing aid. The other is a done-for-you AI SEO service platform.

Does content optimization alone get you cited by AI?

Sometimes — but it's usually half the job. Well-structured content with direct answers and clean schema genuinely helps AI models extract and quote you. But if credible sites don't mention your brand, no amount of on-page polish moves the needle.

We've seen this play out with real content. Free Room Planner, a browser-based floor planning tool, ran an automated AEO content programme through Ralf. From a standing start on 20 June 2026, 69 articles were live by 30 July. By 29 July, 9 of those articles had earned 33 citations in AI answers, with the fastest first citation landing 13.6 days after publishing and the average around 43 days.

Be clear about what that shows. It's an early result, and 60 of the 69 articles hadn't been cited yet. The site's product pages always earned far more citations than the new content did. The point isn't that content is magic — it's that structured content plus measurement plus consistent publishing produces citations you can actually track, which a brief-and-grade tool never surfaces.

Why is my website not showing up in AI responses?

Usually because AI models don't have enough clear, corroborated signals to trust and extract your content. The common causes are thin structure, weak or inconsistent entity data, and — most often — too few credible external mentions pointing at you.

A content optimizer can help with the first. It can't diagnose the second or fix the third. To find out why you're invisible, you need a tool that reads AI answers directly, not one that reads Google's SERP. How to choose between Frase and an AEO platform

Match the tool to the actual problem. Ask what you're trying to fix.

  • You need better briefs for a Google-ranking content team. Frase is a solid, affordable choice and probably enough.
  • You want to know if AI models cite you, and why not. You need AEO measurement Frase doesn't offer.
  • You want the gaps found and fixed without staffing it. You need an automation platform that executes — content, structure, and outreach — not another dashboard.

Many teams keep both. Frase drafts, an AEO platform measures and executes. But if budget forces a choice and AI visibility is the goal, a writing grader won't get you there. For agencies juggling this across clients, we've mapped the options in AI SEO tools for agencies.

How AEO measurement compares to traditional crawling

Traditional tools crawl your site and report on structure, links, and indexing. Useful, but they measure the site — not the answers. AEO platforms measure the output: what an AI model says when someone asks about your category.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions teams ask when comparing Frase against AEO automation platforms.

Does Frase.io have an AEO feature?

Frase has published guidance on FAQ schema and AI search, but its core product is SEO content briefs and draft grading, not AEO measurement or execution. It does not track where you appear in AI answers or tell you why you're missing from them.

Can I use Frase and an AEO platform together?

Yes, and many teams do. Frase handles brief-building and on-page content structure, while a dedicated AEO platform measures AI citations and executes the off-page work. They solve different halves of the problem rather than competing directly.

How do I track my brand mentions across all major AI chatbots?

You need a platform that queries ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity directly and records where your brand is cited versus your competitors. Content optimization tools like Frase can't do this because they read Google SERP data, not AI-generated answers.

What makes content more likely to be cited by AI?

Direct answers near the top, clear entity definitions, clean structured data, quotable statistics with named sources, and — crucially — credible external sites mentioning your brand. On-page structure helps extraction; off-page authority earns trust. You need both.

Is Frase worth it for AI search visibility specifically?

For Google-focused content it's a reasonable value. For AI search visibility specifically, it falls short because it neither measures AI citations nor executes the fixes that earn them. If AEO is your goal, an automation platform is the better fit.
