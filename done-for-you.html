Questions

FAQ

How does the $500 monthly charge work?
It is the minimum monthly commitment for Done For You. It is used against the individual link placements you approve. Publisher pricing varies by website, and we show you the exact page and negotiated price before committing any money.
What happens if I want more than $500 of links?
Set the number of links or monthly budget you are targeting during onboarding. We will source enough relevant opportunities and arrange the additional approved placement cost with you. There are no artificial Starter, Growth or Scale bundles.
How is this different from Ralf Outreach?
Ralf Outreach is self-serve software. Done For You is the operated version: we map targets, contact publishers, follow up, negotiate, manage publisher payment and verify the live placement for you.
Is this a link swap or link exchange?
No. We do not ask you to add outbound links in return, exchange links between customers or place links through a reciprocal network. Every opportunity is sourced because the page is relevant to your competitors, category or industry.
Do you send mass outreach?
No. Each pitch is tied to a specific article and a specific reason your business belongs on it. We prioritise relevant publisher conversations instead of generic emails to thousands of unrelated websites.
What if I do not like an opportunity?
Skip it. You only pay toward the exact placements you approve. We do not force a weaker link merely to hit a monthly count.
Can this work alongside my SEO agency?
Yes. Your agency can continue handling strategy, content and technical SEO while Ralf handles targeted publisher outreach and link placement.
Do I have to commit long term?
No. The minimum commitment renews monthly. You can cancel future renewals, and every individual placement still requires your approval.
