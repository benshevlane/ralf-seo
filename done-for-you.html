How it works

The labour-intensive work happens before anything reaches you. Your role is deliberately small: review a qualified page and its agreed price, then approve or skip.

We start with your named competitors, then identify relevant roundups, comparisons and industry resources where your brand genuinely belongs.

Questions

FAQ

How is this different from Ralf Outreach?
Ralf Outreach is the self-serve software product: your team uses it to manage opportunities and outreach. Done For You is the operated version. We research targets, contact publishers, follow up, negotiate and manage approved placements for you. You pay through a monthly link-placement budget rather than for software access alone.
Is this a link swap or link exchange?
No. We do not ask you to add outbound links in return, exchange links between customers or place links through a reciprocal network. Every opportunity is sourced because the page is relevant to your category, your competitors or your industry.
Do you send mass outreach?
No. Each pitch is tied to a specific article and a specific reason your business belongs on it. We prioritise smaller numbers of relevant publisher conversations rather than sending generic emails to thousands of unrelated websites.
Do pages have to mention a competitor?
Competitor mentions are the strongest starting signal, but not the only one. We also approach relevant roundups, comparison pages, industry resources and buying guides where your inclusion is editorially sensible, even when a named competitor is not already present.
What if I do not like an opportunity?
Skip it. No placement budget is used unless you approve the exact article and negotiated price. Unused budget can roll over for up to three months.
How many links will I get for $500?
Typically one to two, depending on publisher pricing and the strength of the available targets. The exact page and cost are always shown before approval, so link quality and relevance are not sacrificed to hit an artificial monthly count.
Can this work alongside my SEO agency?
Yes. Your agency can continue handling strategy, content and technical SEO while Ralf handles targeted publisher outreach and link placement. There is no need to replace the rest of your SEO team.
Do I have to commit long term?
No. The service is month to month. You can cancel future renewals at any time, and every individual placement still requires your approval.
